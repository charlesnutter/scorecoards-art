About this tool

Data comes live from the public MLB Stats API. Team lists are era-correct for the chosen season. Hits show 1B/2B/3B/HR; outs show fielding credits (6-3, F7). Base paths trace advancement from later plays, and runs scored are marked on the plate appearance where the runner reached.
